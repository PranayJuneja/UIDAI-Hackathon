# Online Hackathon on Data-Driven Innovation on Aadhaar - 2026

**Comprehensive Guide & Breakdown**

This document details every aspect of the "Online Hackathon on Data-Driven Innovation on Aadhaar 2026" organized by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) in association with NIC and MeitY.

## 1. Core Objective & Problem Statement

**Theme:** Unlocking Societal Trends in Aadhaar Enrolment and Updates

**Goal:**
The primary objective is to use anonymized Aadhaar enrolment and update datasets to:
- Identify meaningful patterns, trends, and anomalies.
- Find predictive indicators.
- Translate these findings into clear insights or solution frameworks.
- Support informed decision-making and system improvements for Aadhaar.

**Purpose:**
To leverage data analytics to find "meaningful findings" that potentially have social or administrative benefits.

---

## 2. Participation & Eligibility

### Who Can Participate?
- **Students & Researchers:** Open to any student enrolled in any accredited Indian University or Institution.
- **Age limit:** Must be at least **18 years of age** as of **1st January 2026**.
- **Teams:**
    - Individual participation is allowed.
    - Teams can be formed with a **maximum of 5 members** (including the Team Lead).
    - Ideally heterogeneous teams with diverse skill sets.
    - **One Team Rule:** A participant can only join **one team**.

### Who Cannot Participate?
- People associated with service delivery at **UIDAI** and **NIC** are actively excluded.
- Employees of UIDAI are not eligible.

---

## 3. Registration Process

**Platform:** [JanParichay (MeriPehchaan)](https://janparichay.meripehchaan.gov.in)

**Steps:**
1.  **Access Challenge Page:** Go to [event.data.gov.in](https://event.data.gov.in/challenge/uidai-data-hackathon-2026/) and click "Login to Participate".
2.  **Redirect to JanParichay:** You will be redirected to the JanParichay platform.
    - **New Users:** Must sign up/register using a **Mobile Number**.
    - **Existing Users:** Can login using Username/Password, Mobile/Password, or Email/Password.
3.  **Update Email (Crucial):**
    - It is **mandatory** to have an updated email ID in your JanParichay account.
    - If missing, go to `Edit Profile` -> `Verification Details` -> Select `Primary Email Id` -> Verify with OTP.
4.  **Team Creation:**
    - Any member can create the team.
    - The Team Lead must submit their student ID card along with those of all team members.

---

## 4. Hackathon Structure & Timeline

- **Format:** Primarily **Online**.
- **Duration:** **15 Days** (from the launch of the Hackathon for registration and idea submission).
- **Process:**
    1.  **Registration & Submission:** Online phase.
    2.  **Shortlisting:** Teams will be shortlisted based on submissions.
    3.  **Final Presentation:** Shortlisted teams will present to UIDAI officials and Jury.
    4.  **Award Ceremony:** In-person event at **Delhi or Bengaluru** (Travel expenses reimbursed by UIDAI).

---

## 5. Submission Requirements

Participants must submit **one consolidated PDF** containing the following four sections:

1.  **Problem Statement & Approach:**
    - Concise description of the specific problem being addressed.
    - Proposed analytical or technical approach.

2.  **Datasets Used:**
    - Clear description of datasets and columns used.
    - **Mandatory:** Must use Aadhaar enrolment and/or update datasets provided by UIDAI.
    - **Provided Data:**
        - Enrolment Data
        - Demographic Update Data
        - Biometric Update Data

3.  **Methodology:**
    - Detailed explanation of cleaning, preprocessing, and transformations applied to the data.

4.  **Data Analysis & Visualization:**
    - Key findings and insights.
    - Visualizations/Infographics.
    - **Code Included:** Include code files or notebooks used for analysis **within the PDF itself**.

> **Note:** Shortlisted teams may be asked to submit code files/notebooks separately on GitHub later.

---

## 6. Technology Stack & Rules

- **Allowed Tools:** Any programming language or analytics tool (Python, R, SQL, Excel, Tableau, Power BI, etc.).
- **Emerging Tech:** Use of AI/ML is encouraged.
- **Open Source:** Use of open-source packages is recommended.
- **Code Integrity:** Submitted code must be free from malware (adware, ransomware, virus, etc.).
- **Originality:** must be original work created specifically for this Hackathon. Previously published/awarded work is **not allowed**.

---

## 7. Evaluation Criteria (The Jury)

Submissions are rated on 5 parameters:

1.  **Data Analysis & Insights:**
    - Depth/Accuracy of Univariate, Bivariate, Trivariate analysis.
    - Ability to extract meaningful findings.
2.  **Creativity & Originality:**
    - Uniqueness of problem statement/solution.
    - Innovative use of datasets.
3.  **Technical Implementation:**
    - Code quality, reproducibility, rigor of approach.
    - Appropriate methods, tooling, and documentation.
4.  **Visualization & Presentation:**
    - Clarity and effectiveness of visualizations.
    - Quality of writen report/slides.
5.  **Impact & Applicability:**
    - Potential for social/administrative benefit.
    - Practicality and feasibility.
